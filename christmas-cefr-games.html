📖 How to Share with Teachers

  1. This is a single self-contained file — no internet needed!
  2. Just send this .html file to other teachers
  3. They save it and double-click to open in any browser
  4. All 4 games + 6 CEFR levels work offline ✅

🎮 How to Play

  • Step 1: Select a CEFR level button (A1 = easiest, C2 = most advanced)
  • Step 2: Click a game tab → choose difficulty level (selected button gets white border)
  • 🧠 Memory: Click cards to flip them — match the icon with its word
  • 🔤 Word Search: Click and drag over letters to select words from the list
  • 📝 Crossword: Read clues → type letters (cursor auto-advances) → click ✅ Check
  • ⛄ Snowman: Read the clue → click letters to guess the word. 6 misses make the snowman melt! Solve all words in the round!
  • 💡 Hint: Reveals one letter (Word Search / Crossword) or one missing letter (Snowman)
  • 🔄 Restart: Generates a fresh puzzle with the same words
